Model A shows substantial degradation at $2\times$ and becomes worse at $3\times$: \(E^{(2)}\) increases from 0.0189 to 0.0443 and 0.0503, respectively. Figure 5 already shows blur at $1\times$; at faster speeds, digit shapes deteriorate further. At $3\times$, the predicted 4 changes little between forecasts while its ground-truth position continues changing. This suggests underestimated motion, while the blur suggests uncertainty about shape and position.

The degradation at $2\times$ occurs despite its median speed reference of 6.64 pixels/frame being below the estimated eight-pixel reach per axis. Thus, reach is an upper bound on information movement, not a guarantee of successful forecasting. The estimate does not capture unfamiliar training speeds, and a median does not bound every displacement.
