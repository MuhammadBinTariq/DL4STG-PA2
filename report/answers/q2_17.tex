The final model has learned some useful motion patterns, but not a reliable general motion rule. Its $E^{(2)}$ at $3\times$ decreases from model A's 0.0503 to 0.0324, at a cost to original $1\times$ accuracy. However, Figure 6 still shows blurry predictions and loss of digit identity. Exact mirror/rotation consistency is guaranteed by the wrapper and therefore does not establish learned motion. Stronger evidence would be sharp forecasts that maintain digit identity and correct trajectories through overlaps and bounces under unseen conditions. Generalisation to speeds beyond the trained $1\times$-$3\times$ range remains uncertain.
