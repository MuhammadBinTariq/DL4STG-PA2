Figure 1(a) shows samples in every direction range, but the bars have different heights. Thus, directions are broadly represented without being equally common. Mirroring or rotation can change their relative frequencies. The transformed motion directions would therefore likely be familiar, although this does not guarantee successful forecasting.

From Figure 1(b), a typical speed is 3-4 pixels/frame, with a median of 3.32. Scaling the unrounded median gives nominal references of 6.64 and 9.95 pixels per observed step at $2\times$ and $3\times$. These are scaled references, not separately measured flow medians.

Where digits overlap, one displacement vector may not represent either digit's motion accurately. This could bias both histograms and the median. The size of this bias was not quantified, so the conclusions remain tentative.
